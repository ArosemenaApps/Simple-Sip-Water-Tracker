Simple Sip — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Simple Sip is built around a simple idea: your hydration data is yours. This policy explains what the app does — and, more importantly, what it doesn't do.

We do not collect your data

Simple Sip does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We run no servers, and nothing you log is ever sent to us.

Your hydration data

Your water entries and daily goal are stored locally on your iPhone and, if you use one, your paired Apple Watch. Your daily goal stays on each device and is not synced. Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange your totals and quick-add buttons directly, using Apple's built-in Watch communication. If you have iCloud backup enabled, your device may include this data in your personal, encrypted device backup — that backup belongs to you and is never visible to us.

If you are signed in to iCloud, Simple Sip keeps your quick-add buttons (their names, amounts, icons, colors and order) and your preferred unit in step across your iPhones using Apple's iCloud key-value storage in your own iCloud account. No hydration history, totals, goals or Health data are stored there, and we cannot see it.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health sync, Simple Sip writes the water you log to the Health app as Dietary Water, and reads water data to keep your history in sync — including water you logged with Simple Sip on another iPhone or Apple Watch, so your history comes back when you switch phones. This happens only with your explicit permission, entirely on your device, using Apple's HealthKit framework. Health data is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is never used for advertising or marketing. Apple Health may sync your Health data across your own devices through your Apple-managed iCloud and Health settings; that is handled by Apple, not by Simple Sip. You can revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Notifications

Hydration reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

Purchases

Premium and tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchase records are managed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Simple Sip does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. The app is rated 4+ and contains no third-party content.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, we expect changes to be rare.
